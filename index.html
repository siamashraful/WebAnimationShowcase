What's inside

Every element on this page moves with intent.

Spring physics

Buttons and cards respond with overshoot easing — the bouncy, weighted feel Framer Motion made famous, recreated with cubic-bezier springs.

Magnetic pull

Hover near the hero buttons and they lean toward your cursor, then snap back with a spring when you leave. Pure pointer math.

3D tilt cards

These cards track your cursor in 3D space with perspective rotation and a glow that follows the pointer — move your mouse across one.

Scroll choreography

Sections fade, slide, zoom and flip into view as you scroll, with staggered children — driven by IntersectionObserver, zero scroll-jank.

Parallax depth

The dark band below moves its shapes at different speeds as you scroll, creating layered depth like a Framer scroll-transform section.

Velocity skew

The giant headlines near the bottom skew and slide proportionally to your scroll speed — scroll fast and watch them shear.

Depth is just speed.
