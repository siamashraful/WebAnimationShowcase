Detector + reviewer independently converged: eyebrow kickers ×4 (`repeated-section-kickers`), gradient text ×4–6 (stat numbers least defensible; hero/tooltip functionally justified), identical icon-card grid ×2 (behavior-justified, invisible in a screenshot), hero-metric stats template (content justified, stock form), glassmorphism (register-justified).
